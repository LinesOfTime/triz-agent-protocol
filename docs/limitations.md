# Limitations and responsible use

- An LLM can produce a plausible but false contradiction.
- Functional models depend on analyst-selected boundaries.
- Cause graphs remain hypotheses until their links are supported.
- Pattern catalogues can cause premature anchoring.
- A highly scored concept can still be unsafe, unlawful, or infeasible.

The project does not provide engineering certification, medical or legal advice, patentability opinions, safety approval, or authorization to modify live systems.

Version 0.1 validates structure and internal consistency, not inventiveness or factual correctness. Effectiveness claims require benchmark results and expert review.

The repository contains original summaries, schemas, workflows, and adaptations. It excludes reproduced matrices, inventive-standard texts, proprietary courses, and copied case studies.
