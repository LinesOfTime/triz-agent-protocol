# TRIZ-Lite

Return: problem, evidence and assumptions, goal, invariants, IFR, primary contradiction, resources, at least two solution mechanisms, verification plan, open questions, and the smallest reversible next step.

Do not substitute a list of inventive principles for establishing the contradiction.

When evidence is distributed across a repository, begin with the task, governing instructions, current state, and directly affected files. Expand through observed references and unresolved critical claims; consult history only when current evidence does not resolve a conflict.
